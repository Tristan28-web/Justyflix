Automatic Default English Audio Setup

Dual Audio releases embed both Track 1: Hindi and Track 2: English. You only need to set your media player's preferred audio language once (takes 10 seconds). Afterward, your player will automatically pick English on all Hollywood/Dual Audio movies, while pure Bollywood movies will continue playing in Hindi!

  1. Open VLC Media Player.
  2. Click Tools on the top menu → select Preferences (or press Ctrl + P).
  3. Click the Audio tab at the top.
  4. Scroll down to the Tracks section → look for Preferred audio language.
  5. In the text box, type: eng, en, english
  6. Click Save at the bottom right.
  7. Restart VLC. All Hollywood movies will now play in English automatically!
